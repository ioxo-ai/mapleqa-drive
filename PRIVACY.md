# Privacy Policy — Maple QA Drive

Maple QA Drive is a personal tool used only by its owner.

- It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, to upload and download
  Maple QA map and resource archives.
- It does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data or Google user
  data with third parties.
- OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own machines and CI secrets.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
